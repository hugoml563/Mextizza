A rubber stamp for process claims on packaging, cards and social pieces.

```jsx
<Stamp lines={['Hecho a', 'mano', 'en 48h']} />
<Stamp lines={['Fermento', '48h']} color="var(--rosa-mexicano)" size={110} tilt={-3} />
```

Keep the copy to three short words per line — it must read as a stamp, not a paragraph.
